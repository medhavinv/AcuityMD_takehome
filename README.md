# AcuityMD PM Take-Home

## The Assignment

**Role:** Single PM at a wedding planning collaboration startup.

**The product today** has three features: guest list management, seating charts, and to-do tracking.

**The problem:** Usage is declining. Planners and guests are increasingly turning to general-purpose AI (Claude, ChatGPT) to do the work the product used to own.

**The ask from the CEO:** Propose a solution.

---

## Deliverables

### 1. PRD (`PRD.md`)
Max 300 words. Captures the problem, proposed solution, and the thinking behind it — including assumptions.

### 2. Prototype (this repo)
A clickable or visual prototype with enough fidelity to communicate the experience to design, engineering, and marketing.

---

## Interview Format

Live walkthrough of both artifacts with a panel:
- **Designer** — experience, fidelity, interaction decisions
- **Engineering Manager** — feasibility, scope, technical trade-offs
- **Product Marketer** — positioning, differentiation, go-to-market angle

Panel will ask about approach, trade-offs, and how you'd bring the team along.

---

## Ground Rules

- Make any assumptions needed — just capture them
- Optimize for **clarity of thinking** over polish
- Use whatever tools help you move fast
